How to take useful photos

  • Clean the car where practical and photograph it in daylight with even lighting.
  • Use sharp, unfiltered images showing the actual vehicle; avoid misleading edits or hiding defects.
  • Photograph scratches, dents, tyre wear and other known issues clearly.
  • Use a safe, permitted location; never take pictures while driving.
  • Only share photographs you own or have permission to use. Remove unrelated people, personal addresses, documents and location metadata where appropriate.
  • Do not publish full registration certificates, identity documents, insurance policy numbers or other sensitive information.

Publication policy: Sending a seller enquiry does not automatically publish a listing. Vehicle details and any proposed images require manual review and appropriate seller permission. Photos are not proof of condition, ownership or availability.
