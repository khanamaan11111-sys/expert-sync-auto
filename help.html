Frequently asked questions

Are all cars shown on the site currently available?

No. The Explore Cars page shows enquiry categories, not actual stock. The Available Cars page only displays approved published records; confirm current availability with our contact before proceeding.

Can I sell my car through Expert Sync Auto?

You can submit a selling enquiry using our Sell Your Car form. Submission does not guarantee a sale or automatically publish a listing.

Is the EMI calculator a loan offer?

No. It is an illustrative calculation based on values you enter. Actual loan eligibility, rates and charges depend on a lender.

Are listed vehicles inspected or certified?

Do not assume so. Verify ownership, documents, condition, history, pricing and other claims independently before making a decision.

Does sending an enquiry require payment?

There is no website checkout or enquiry payment mechanism in the current version. Confirm any future commercial terms directly and obtain appropriate documentation before making a payment.

How should I protect myself when buying a used car?

Inspect the car in person, verify its documentation and ownership, check any outstanding finance or legal restrictions, and use traceable payment methods. Avoid sending deposits based only on an online message. Use our free used car buying checklist as a practical guide.

Safety reminder: Never share OTPs, passwords, payment PINs or identity documents through a public enquiry. Do not pay an unknown party solely to reserve a vehicle. This website does not collect online payments.
