Before selecting a policy

  • Confirm the insurer and intermediary through official channels and read the actual policy wording.
  • Check the coverage type, exclusions, insured declared value, deductible and applicable add-ons.
  • Review claim conditions, cashless network details, no-claim bonus eligibility and renewal dates.
  • Do not pay through an unverified link or share OTPs, passwords or UPI PINs.

Important: The lowest premium is not necessarily the best protection. This worksheet is educational, not insurance advice, a quotation, policy issuance or a purchase facility.
