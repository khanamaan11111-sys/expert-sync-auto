Privacy & Website Terms

Information about using our current enquiry website.

This page describes the present static website and its features. It is general operational information, not a substitute for a legally reviewed privacy notice or commercial agreement when the business expands.

1. What this website does

Expert Sync Auto provides car-discovery information, vehicle enquiry categories, a seller enquiry form, an approved-listings catalogue, a comparison tool and an illustrative EMI calculator. An enquiry is not a confirmed sale, purchase, reservation or financing offer.

2. Information you choose to share

The enquiry forms prepare WhatsApp messages using information you enter, such as your name, car preferences, seller enquiry details or question. The current static website does not operate its own submission database. When WhatsApp opens, you can review and edit the message before choosing to send it. Any message you send is handled through WhatsApp and received by the automotive contact.

3. Local browser tools

The EMI calculator processes entered amounts in your browser. Listing-preparation and review utilities process drafts in your browser and can download files to your device. These pages are not secured admin dashboards and should never be used for private seller documents or credentials.

4. External services and hosting

The website may be hosted through GitHub Pages and contains links to WhatsApp and telephone calling. Those providers may process information under their own applicable terms and privacy practices. We do not control their systems.

5. Vehicle listing information

Only seller-authorized, reviewed records should be published. Listing details, prices and availability can change. Buyers should independently check vehicle condition, ownership, documents, legal restrictions and other material facts before proceeding. No inspection, certification or guarantee is implied unless explicitly supported.

6. Payments and financing

The current website does not have an online checkout or collect card details, payment PINs or OTPs. The EMI calculator is illustrative and does not constitute lending, brokerage, eligibility assessment or a lender quotation. Do not send advance payments to an unknown party based solely on an online message.

7. Contact and requests

For questions about an enquiry or information you have voluntarily shared with our automotive contact, call +91 9340196039 or use WhatsApp. Do not send passwords, OTPs, payment credentials or identity documents through the public enquiry forms.

8. Updates

Website features and this information may be revised as the service develops. Any future account system, analytics, payments or backend storage should be accompanied by an updated notice and appropriate controls before launch.
