Buyer and Seller Safety Center

Practical precautions for vehicle enquiries, meetings, payments and ownership transfer.

Important: Expert Sync Auto enquiries do not confirm a booking, vehicle availability, identity verification or payment. Independently verify the other party, the vehicle and the transaction.

Before a viewing

  • Agree on a sensible public meeting location and tell someone you trust your plans.
  • Check vehicle identity and documents against available official records.
  • Ask permission before inspecting, photographing or test driving.
  • Do not send identity documents or sensitive financial information to unknown contacts.

Payment precautions

  • Confirm the recipient and written terms before transferring money.
  • Never share OTPs, UPI PINs, passwords or remote-access permissions.
  • Do not rely on screenshots, SMS claims or a caller saying a transfer is complete.
  • Check received funds through your own bank before releasing the vehicle or keys.

Vehicle and document checks

  • Compare registration and chassis/engine details with the physical vehicle and paperwork.
  • Clarify outstanding finance, insurance, pending dues and transfer responsibilities.
  • Consider an independent mechanic inspection before purchase.
  • Confirm current RTO and insurer requirements directly with the relevant organisations.

Handover precautions

  • Write down the agreed price, date, included accessories and pending work.
  • Retain appropriate receipts and a mutually acknowledged handover record.
  • Follow through on ownership transfer and insurance changes promptly.
  • Keep copies of relevant records securely; avoid publicly posting personal documents.
